The Library Exhibition in 1933
Reconstruction from the Earthquake


In 1933, the Library Exhibition was held at the Tokyo Imperial University Library. According to the catalog, the exhibition divided into 9 categories, dispalying a lot of special collections donated from Japan and abroad. The exhibition space consisted of the first to third floors using the grand staircase of the new library.  








The First Floor of the Library


"Kinen-shitsu" on the first floor was 317 square meters big. Former presidents' photos, graduation certificates, 50 year’s history, foreign professors' photos and publications of the university were exhibited in this special room. 

The Second Floor


"Chinretsu-shitsu" on the second floor was 274 square meters big. As a main exhibition hall, this room displayed many rare books in the 1933 library exhibition.

The Third Floor

Nowadays we can search library books through OPAC, but there used to be Japanese and Western Library Cards around the grand  staircase on the third floor.

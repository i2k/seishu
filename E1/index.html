Reconstruction after the Great Kanto
Earthquake  

On September 1st, 1923, a magnitude 7.9 earthquake struck the Tokyo region. The Great Kanto Earthquake has caused most of the University of Tokyo buildings to collapse. The University Library was burned down by a fire and a huge collection of books, about 700,000, was destroyed. The university  established a reconstruction committee and started activities. Starting with "Seishu Bunko" and "Nanki Bunko" which is a private library of Japan, the library accepted donations of books from over 30 countries. The year after the earthquake, John Rockefeller, Jr. donated for the reconstruction of the University Library. The new library designed by Professor Yoshikazu Uchida was built in 1928. There is a fountain in front of the present General Library, built for fire prevention in response to the request of Rockefeller, Jr.  Most of the special collections were temporarily evacuated to the storehouse of "Seishu Bunko" in Yamanashi prefecture in 1944 wartime. These valuable books are now stored in the General Library as  cultural resources,  passing on the memories of the library history.（「文化資源としての書物」履修生）
